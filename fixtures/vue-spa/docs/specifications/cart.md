# Cart

**Category:** web

## Status

Built.

## Behaviour

- The cart badge in the header shows the **total quantity** of items in the cart (two of one book and one of
  another shows `3`). An empty cart shows no badge.
- A line total is `price × quantity` in euros, formatted with two decimals, **rounding half up on cents**
  (`1.005 × 1` → `1.01`).
